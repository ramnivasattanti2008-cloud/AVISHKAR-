# Canonical Data Dictionary — UrjaSetu EMS

This dictionary defines every supported physical and derived energy metric within UrjaSetu EMS,
specifying exact measurement definitions, canonical units, measurement kinds, valid ranges, and validation invariants.

---

## 1. Supported Metric Definitions

| Metric ID | Metric Name | Definition | Canonical Unit | Measurement Kind | Valid Range | Physical Invariants |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ENERGY_REQUIREMENT_MU` | Energy Requirement | Total electrical energy required by the grid system during the reporting period, including transmission and distribution losses. | `MU` | `PERIOD_TOTAL_MU` | [0.0, 500,000.0] | Must be non-negative. Strictly an Energy quantity. |
| `ENERGY_SUPPLIED_MU` | Energy Supplied | Total electrical energy actually delivered to consumers and distribution systems by generators. | `MU` | `PERIOD_TOTAL_MU` | [0.0, 500,000.0] | Must be non-negative. $\le$ Energy Requirement under normal grid deficit. |
| `PEAK_DEMAND_MW` | Peak Electricity Demand | Maximum instantaneous electrical power demanded from the grid during the reporting period. | `kW` (normalized) / `MW` | `INSTANTANEOUS_KW` | [0.0, 500,000.0] | Must be non-negative. Instantaneous power, not energy. |
| `PEAK_MET_MW` | Peak Demand Met | Maximum instantaneous electrical power successfully dispatched and met by the system. | `kW` (normalized) / `MW` | `INSTANTANEOUS_KW` | [0.0, 500,000.0] | $\le$ Peak Demand. Power quantity. |
| `SOLAR_CAPACITY_MW` | Installed Solar Capacity | Cumulative nameplate DC/AC rating of grid-connected solar power plants and registered rooftop systems. | `MW` | `NAMEPLATE_CAPACITY_MW` | [0.0, 250,000.0] | Monotonically non-decreasing except under verified decommissioning. |
| `SOLAR_GENERATION_MU` | Solar Energy Generation | Total electrical energy generated by grid-connected and registered solar installations. | `MU` | `PERIOD_TOTAL_MU` | [0.0, 50,000.0] | Must be non-negative. Energy quantity. |
| `TOTAL_GENERATION_MU` | Total Generation | Total electrical energy produced by all generation sources (thermal, hydro, nuclear, renewable). | `MU` | `PERIOD_TOTAL_MU` | [0.0, 500,000.0] | $\ge$ Solar Generation. |
| `RENEWABLE_GENERATION_MU`| Total Renewable Generation | Energy produced from solar, wind, biomass, and small hydro sources. | `MU` | `PERIOD_TOTAL_MU` | [0.0, 100,000.0] | $\ge$ Solar Generation; $\le$ Total Generation. |
| `HOUSEHOLD_CONSUMPTION_KWH`| Household Energy Consumption | Electrical energy consumed by a residential premise in a given interval (15m, 1h, or monthly). | `kWh` | `INTERVAL_KWH` | [0.0, 10,000.0] | Must be non-negative. |

## 2. Measurement Kinds

1. `INSTANTANEOUS_KW`: Instantaneous rate of energy flow (Power). Cannot be integrated without duration.
2. `INTERVAL_KWH`: Discrete energy volume accumulated within a bounded time window (e.g. 15-minute interval).
3. `CUMULATIVE_KWH`: Monotonically increasing meter register. Consumption = $Reading_{t_2} - Reading_{t_1}$.
4. `PERIOD_TOTAL_MU`: Monthly, quarterly, or annual total energy volume in Million Units ($1\text{ MU} = 10^6\text{ kWh} = 1\text{ GWh}$).
5. `NAMEPLATE_CAPACITY_MW`: Rated maximum output power under standard test conditions. Not an energy quantity.
6. `RATIO_PERCENT`: Dimensionless share or proportion bounded between 0% and 100%.

---

## 3. Strict Dimensional Rules & Invariants

```
Power (kW) != Energy (kWh)
Power (MW) != Energy (MU / GWh)

Energy (kWh) = Power (kW) * Time (hours)
Energy (MU)  = Power (MW) * Time (hours) / 1000

Forbidden Operations:
1. convert_units("100 kW", "kWh") -> Raises TypeError
2. calculate_solar_share("10,000 MU", "82,000 MW Installed") -> Raises TypeError
3. aggregate_state_to_district("State Total" / 31) -> Raises ValueError
```
